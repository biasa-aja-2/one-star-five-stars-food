> One star: your species queues for lukewarm bean water, yells at glowing rectangles, and charges rent for sleeping — but the dumplings alone are worth the 40-light-year trip.

One sentence (173 chars, inside the 1–200 gate) in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment about the food.

## Why this line

The complaint is three specific, baffled alien observations — queuing for lukewarm bean water (coffee), yelling at glowing rectangles (phones), paying rent just to sleep — each a human habit that looks absurd from outside, and together a grievance worth exactly one star. Then the em-dash turn lands the food compliment: the dumplings alone justify a 40-light-year trip. The joke is the scale mismatch — a whole planet written off, then redeemed by one dish — and the alien voice never breaks: "your species", not "you guys".

python3 main.py
